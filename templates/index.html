💡 Understanding Email Classification: Spam vs Ham

A quick beginner guide on email terminology and safety
🚨

What is SPAM?

Spam refers to unsolicited, unwanted, or harmful bulk messages. It often includes fraudulent financial requests, phishing scams to steal passwords, fake lottery wins, or deceptive advertising.

  • Contains suspicious links or urgent demands for money
  • Unrecognized senders promising unrealistic rewards
  • Grammatical anomalies or spoofed domains
✉️

What is HAM?

Ham is the computer science term for legitimate, non-spam email. These are normal communications that the recipient expects or wants to receive, such as personal chats, work emails, or receipts.

  • Work correspondence, meeting notes, project updates
  • Personal conversations with colleagues and family
  • Expected newsletters, order receipts, and confirmations
